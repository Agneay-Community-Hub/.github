## 👋 Who we are

We're a crew of curious, hands-on engineers who love turning wild ideas into working prototypes under tight deadlines. Game jams and hackathons are our playground — we learn fast, ship fast, and have a lot of fun doing it.

## 💡 What drives us

- **Learn by building** — every jam is a chance to pick up a new engine, tool, or skill.
- **Ship it** — a finished, playable thing beats a perfect idea on paper.
- **Build together** — we share code, assets, and lessons openly.
